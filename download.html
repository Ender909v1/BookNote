BookNote

Keep books, fields and cards with images, notes and tags. Everything is saved on your own device. No account needed.

Open BookNote

PC (Windows / Mac)

Use Chrome or Edge.

Install as an app (recommended)
  1. Open BookNote in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click Install app at the top of the page (or the install icon at the right of the address bar).
  3. BookNote gets its own window and a Start menu / desktop icon, and works offline.
Or download the files
  1. Download the ZIP below and unzip it (for example into Documents).
  2. Double-click index.html to open it.
Download for PC (.zip)

Android

Use Chrome.

  1. Open this page on your phone in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app on this device above. If you don't see it, tap Chrome's ⋮ menu → Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. BookNote appears in your app list with its own icon and works offline.
Open BookNote on this phone

Your notes stay on each device. The PC app, the phone app and the downloaded files each keep their own data. To move your books between them, use Backup all on one and Import on the other.
